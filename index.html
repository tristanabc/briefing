Scientists crack "undruggable" pancreatic cancer target with new KRAS drug

Researchers have developed daraxonrasib, targeting the KRAS mutation that drives the majority of pancreatic cancers — a mutation long considered impossible to block. Pancreatic cancer has one of the lowest survival rates of any cancer; this could be a significant turning point.

Why I picked this: KRAS has been called "undruggable" for decades. This is the kind of "finally cracked it" moment that genuinely matters for millions of patients.

NASA / Science News
Webb telescope detects methane on interstellar comet 3I/ATLAS — a first for a visitor from another star

The James Webb Space Telescope has identified methane in interstellar comet 3I/ATLAS — the first time the molecule has been directly detected on an object from outside our solar system. The chemistry hints at conditions in an entirely different planetary system.

Why I picked this: We're literally reading the chemistry of another star system from an object that fell into ours — science fiction made real.
