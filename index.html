Webb telescope detects methane on interstellar comet 3I/ATLAS — a first for any visitor from another star

The James Webb Space Telescope identified methane in interstellar comet 3I/ATLAS — the first time the molecule has been directly detected on an object from outside our solar system. Only the third interstellar object ever found, it's giving scientists their first real chemical fingerprint of another planetary system.

Why I picked this: We're reading the chemistry of another star system from an object that fell into ours — science fiction made real, and it happened this week.

Octopuses can use mirrors to find hidden food — joining an exclusive vertebrate-only club

Dartmouth researchers showed octopuses can use mirror reflections to locate food hidden from direct view — previously only documented in vertebrates like apes, dolphins, and corvids. Octopuses share no common ancestor with any of those animals, making the convergent evolution genuinely puzzling.

Why I picked this: Every octopus cognition study is a gift. Independent evolution of sophisticated visual reasoning in a mollusc has no satisfying explanation yet.
