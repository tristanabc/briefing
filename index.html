NASA / Science News
Webb telescope detects methane on interstellar comet 3I/ATLAS — a first for any visitor from another star

The James Webb Space Telescope has identified methane in interstellar comet 3I/ATLAS — the first time the molecule has been directly detected on an object from outside our solar system. Only the third interstellar object ever found, it's giving scientists their first real chemical read on another planetary system.

Why I picked this: We're reading the chemistry of another star system from an object that fell into ours — science fiction made real, and it happened this week.

Science News / Dartmouth
Octopuses learn to use mirrors to find hidden food — joining a very exclusive vertebrate club

Dartmouth researchers showed octopuses can use mirror reflections to locate food hidden from their direct line of sight — a skill previously documented only in vertebrates like apes, dolphins, and corvids. Octopuses share no common ancestor with any of those animals, which makes the convergent evolution even stranger.

Why I picked this: Every octopus cognition study is a gift. Independent evolution of the same sophisticated visual reasoning in a mollusc is genuinely puzzling science.
